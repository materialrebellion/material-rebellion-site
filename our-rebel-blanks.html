Our Rebel Blanks™

Every Rebel Blank™ begins with waste. Our unique design process is driven by available upcycled materials, making each style and colour combination uniquely resourceful. From working with existing fabric colours to custom dyeing to match your exact Pantone specifications, we create premium merch with purpose. Contact us to explore the possibilities and start your custom order.

  • Our factory partners are all GOTS, FAIRTRADE, UPMADE, OEKO-TEX, and SA 8000 certified.
  • Carbon Cuts — 80–90% fewer emissions vs. new cotton or polyester.
  • Water Savings — ~2,700 L per shirt (≈ 3 years of drinking water).
  • Plastic-Free — natural cotton fibers only; no polyester, no microplastics.
  • Waste Diverted — ~0.27 KGS kept from landfill/incineration per tee.
  • 100% Waste Fabric — no new cotton, every tee made from discarded textiles.
  • Carbon-Neutral Freight — UPS air shipments fully offset.
  • Chemical-Light — no new pesticides, minimal dyes, negligible toxic runoff.
  • Full Impact Reports for ESG/CSR: water / CO₂ / waste saved ETC.

Beyond the Tee

Clothing waste is endless, and so are our possibilities.

We have launched with just our signature rebel tees, but our vision goes far beyond. Thanks to our business model and SOLID factory partnerships, we can apply the same waste diversion methods to all materials and any tech pack: fleece hoodies, plaid overshirts, denim, and more. This isn’t about starting small — it’s about proving scalability, versatility, and a new way forward for sustainable merch.
