Let's create something together

Have an artist, a story, or an idea worth bringing to life? Let’s make something together.

Using production offcuts, surplus fabrics, and overlooked materials, we create one-of-a-kind pieces that connect to your world, tell a story, and give people something they actually want to wear.

  • Beyond the logo. Think limited-edition pieces for tours, pop-ups, exclusive drops, and special collections. Merch that feels more like a statement than a souvenir.
  • Made to be different. From work jackets and sweatshirts to overshirts and beyond, we explore creative possibilities shaped by the materials available.
  • The details make the difference. Quality base fabrics deliver the comfort and function you expect, while reclaimed materials bring unexpected character through pockets, collars, panels, and other design details.
  • No cookie-cutter catalogue. Every collaboration is developed around available materials, design, quantity, and budget. No fixed inventory, just fresh possibilities.

Less waste. More imagination. Something worth wearing.
